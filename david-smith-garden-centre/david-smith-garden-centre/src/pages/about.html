About David Smith’s

A friendly garden centre and nursery at Wilberfoss, near York.

Our garden centre

Plants we’re proud of, and time for our customers

David Smith’s Garden Centre sits on Butts Lane, just off the Hull Road at Wilberfoss, between York and Pocklington.

Walk through our greenhouses and you’ll find benches full of bedding plants, hanging baskets growing on, sunflowers and seasonal pots. Outside there are hydrangeas, shrubs and trees.

Inside the shop there’s compost, pots, tools, pond supplies, loose bird seed and plenty more, including items you won’t find in the big chains. If you need advice, just ask. If it’s heavy, we’ll carry it to your car.
